Reading: on this case both node-muting heuristics reach the oracle's
adoption (regret 0.000) with different mute sets — the oracle is a
score to match, not a unique answer. Edge cutting at the same budget
does nothing here because the claim's genealogy never used the two
hottest pilot edges in the scored run; across the full 40-case grid
edge cutting is the weakest strategy (see results/HEADLINE.md).
